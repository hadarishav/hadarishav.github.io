Selected work

Research that made it into the product.

My work usually starts with a hard-to-measure failure and ends with a model, dataset, or evaluation system that people can use.

01Model training · Safety

Protect

Led the team that trained a multimodal Gemma-3n guardrail on 113K text, image, and audio samples. Protect outperformed GPT-4.1, WildGuard, and LlamaGuard-4 across four safety categories.

  • 96.6 F1 on prompt injection
  • 65 ms median time-to-label
  • LoRA training and ablations on 8×H100 GPUs

02Agents · Evaluation

AgentCompass

Built an evaluation system for finding and explaining failures in agentic workflows. It now runs on live customer workloads and found errors missed by the benchmark’s own annotators.

0.657 on TRAIL error localization, versus 0.546 for Gemini-2.5-Pro.

03LLM-as-judge · Multilingual

Evaluation at Microsoft Research

Built and calibrated multilingual LLM evaluators using 20K native-speaker judgments. Six Microsoft Copilot teams used the framework as a pre-launch quality gate.

Bias beyond a binary label

Studied how gender bias appears in English and Hindi language technology, including normative ratings that capture severity rather than only yes/no labels.
